Basic Care

Watering

Keep the soil moist, but not soggy. Most flowers need regular watering, especially in warm weather.

Sunlight

Give your flowers the amount of sunlight they need for healthy growth.

Soil

Use well-draining soil with enough nutrients to support healthy roots.

Fertilizer

Feed your plants with suitable fertilizer when they need extra nutrients.

Start Small, Grow Big

  • Choose easy-to-grow flowers.
  • Use quality soil and proper drainage.
  • Give your flowers enough sunlight.
  • Water regularly without overwatering.
  • Be patient and enjoy the growing process.
